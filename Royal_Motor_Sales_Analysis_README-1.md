# Royal Motor Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is my **Capstone Project for Royal Solutions Technologies**, focused on analyzing motor vehicle sales data and building an interactive **Power BI dashboard** to uncover sales trends, customer patterns, product performance, and growth over time.

The project involved taking raw sales data through a data-cleaning and transformation process, performing exploratory and business analysis, and presenting the findings through an interactive dashboard.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

- Clean and prepare the raw motor sales dataset.
- Handle missing/null values appropriately.
- Standardize inconsistent text values.
- Replace incorrect or inconsistent values.
- Analyze sales performance across different dimensions.
- Calculate monthly growth rates and variance.
- Identify high-performing car models.
- Analyze sales by gender, location, and age range.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel** – Initial data inspection and preparation
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **Power BI** – Data modeling, DAX calculations, visualization, and dashboard development
- **DAX** – KPI and growth calculations

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Transformation

Several data-preparation steps were performed before analysis, including:

- Handling null/missing values.
- Standardizing text entries for consistency.
- Replacing incorrect or inconsistent values.
- Checking for duplicate or inconsistent records.
- Transforming columns into appropriate data types.
- Creating calculated fields required for analysis.
- Preparing the cleaned dataset for Power BI reporting.

## 📈 Analysis Performed

The analysis covered several business questions and performance indicators.

### Sales Performance

- Total number of cars sold.
- Previous-month sales.
- Sales variance.
- Overall growth rate.
- Monthly sales trends.

### Growth Analysis

Growth analysis was performed to understand changes in sales performance over time.

The dashboard includes:

- Monthly growth rate.
- Month-over-month performance.
- Variance between current and previous periods.
- Growth-rate comparison by location.

### Customer Analysis

Customer-related analysis included:

- Sales by gender.
- Sales by age range.
- Geographic/location analysis.

### Product Analysis

Car model performance was analyzed to identify models with higher sales volumes.

The dashboard provides:

- Total cars sold by car model.
- Comparison of model performance.
- Identification of high- and low-selling models.

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis showed several patterns in the sales data, including:

- Sales performance varied considerably across months.
- Monthly growth rates changed throughout the year, indicating periods of increasing and declining sales.
- Customer sales were distributed across both male and female customers, with male customers accounting for a slightly larger share in the dashboard.
- Some car models recorded significantly higher sales volumes than others.
- Sales performance differed across locations.
- The age-range analysis showed that customers aged **26–30** and **31–35** represented the largest sales groups in the dashboard.
